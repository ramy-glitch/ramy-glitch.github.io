2025

Aligned wrist EDA, BVP, ACC and TEMP from the Empatica E4 for 15 subjects, leaving out the chest sensor. Built the features by hand with NeuroKit2 and tuned a Random Forest with Optuna under 15-fold leave-one-subject-out. On 1,499 windows across baseline, stress, amusement and meditation, macro-F1 was 0.61 against a 1D CNN at 0.51. Stress separated; amusement did not.
